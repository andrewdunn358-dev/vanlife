What do you drive?

The rules depend on the vehicle, not just the place. Pick yours once and every record on this site will say whether it applies to you. Stays in your browser — nothing is sent anywhere.

One more thing, and it is the question that decides most of these: does your vehicle have a fixed toilet and sealed tanks for waste water and sewage?

Authorities that require “self-contained” vehicles almost always mean an onboard toilet plus sealed containers. There is no UK certification for this, so it is a claim rather than a standard — but it is what the bookable bays are checking.
